# Defender abilities, rarity strength and new content

Implemented October 28, 2026.

## Research

- **Rarity:** in Megabonk, upgrade rarity multiplies the stats a level-up gives (Uncommon 1.2x, Rare 1.4x, Epic 1.6x, Legendary 2x) instead of adding levels ([GuruGamer weapon and tome upgrades guide](https://gurugamer.com/pc-console/megabonk-complete-weapon-and-tome-upgrades-guide-25658)). We have three rarities, so Common 1x, Rare 1.5x, Epic 2x.
- **Characters:** each Megabonk character has a starting weapon and an identity passive ([findingdulcinea](https://findingdulcinea.com/best-megabonk-characters-tier-list), [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/megabonk/how-to-unlock-all-characters-in-megabonk/)). Chickenpunch gives each defender a Space ability as well.
- **Tomes and items:** Luck, Thorns and Evasion tomes, plus Anvil, Big Bonk, Forbidden Juice, Ice Crystal, Moldy Cheese and Oats style items ([Dexerto tome tier list](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/megabonk/tomes-tier-list/), [Common Sense Gamer items](https://commonsensegamer.com/megabonk-items/)). We adapt ideas, not numbers.

## Rarity strength

Every pick adds one rank. Rarity adds 1.0, 1.5 or 2.0 to that key's *level*, which drives stat sizes (damage, cooldowns, area, dodge and so on). Counts (eggs, blades, parrots, jabs), evolution requirements and the rank-10 cap still follow rank. Rusty Anvil adds 0.25 level per stack to weapon picks. `run.level(key)` returns the level; `run.offered_gain(key)` previews a pick.

Tomes: Lucky Horseshoe (+4% rare/epic odds per level, better chest odds; Reach level 25), Bramble Coat (attackers take 8 + 6/level damage; 1,000 kills), Feather Step (+4% dodge per level, max 40%; use your ability 50 times in a run), Golden Harvest (+12% coins per level; 10 chests).

Items: Oat Sack (common, +10 HP), Moldy Cheese (rare, poison), Ice Cube (rare, freeze), Hot Sauce (rare, +25% crit damage per stack; Keen Eye 10), Rusty Anvil (epic; win 3 runs), Big Bonk Mallet (epic, 2% chance for 20x; win on Ferocious).

New art: tome, Stink Cloud and evolved icons in `assets/Coop/`, generated by `tools/art/make_content_icons.lua` with editable `.aseprite` sources. The other new art is from the Ninja Adventure pack.

## Balance

- **Bot runs:** on a fresh save the bot survives 2:14–3:23 (six seeds) after a small early buff: Egg Barrage base damage 10 → 13 and Hot Foot base 4 → 5. Before the buff, the rarity change had shortened runs by about 25%.
- **Weapon benchmark at rank 10 (crowd / single-target damage per second):** Pitchfork 121 / 119 (buffed from 82 / 81), Carrot Cannon 217 / 210 (buffed from 130 / 125), Stink Cloud 116 / 59, Parrot Squadron 177 / 120. For reference, Egg Barrage is 346 / 230.
- **Guardian race:** the ladder holds (starter build cannot kill the final guardian; first win 89 s; four slots 22 s). A new broken route — Golden Carrot, Pirate Flock, Big Bonk and Hot Sauce — kills the final guardian in 4.9 s, alongside Overcrit (3.0 s) and Henpocalypse (3.7 s).
